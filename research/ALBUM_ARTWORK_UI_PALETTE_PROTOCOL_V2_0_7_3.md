## Registry And Identity

After coherent-support evidence exists and before lane, obligation, field-hypothesis, representative, or
complete-treatment caps, record every eligible family direction, field-hypothesis root, and legal
foreground or distinct-accent role root. Every entry binds source-connected evidence and deterministic
lineage. Identity obligations are a marked subset, never the registry denominator.

The complete-treatment canonical key remains exactly
`#rrggbb:#rrggbb:#rrggbb:#rrggbb:gradient` or
`#rrggbb:#rrggbb:#rrggbb:#rrggbb:flat`, ordered as background, surface, foreground, accent, and
treatment kind. Every component is lower-case; the first four are six-digit canonical sRGB and the last
is the literal `gradient` or `flat`. Candidate equality, control absence, additions, logical checkpoints,
and transferred evidence use this key, not an implementation-local treatment ID.

## Fixed Arm Order

Every arm starts independently from the same control; arms are not cumulative.

1. `control-0.7.2`: byte-identical control.
2. `all-existing-representative-strategies`: retain `dense-exact`, `nearest-prototype`, and
   `density-synthesized` when source-supported, while preserving the control's same-index field pairing
   rule and every other cap. Generated emergency black or white is unchanged and is not a representative
   strategy.
3. `all-retained-representative-cross-pairs`: enumerate every legal background/surface cross-pair among
   the representatives retained by the control policy, preserving representative-strategy and family
   caps.
4. `widened-field-hypothesis-retention`: carry every eligible existing-semantics field-hypothesis
   proposal through field-conditional role evaluation; preserve control family lanes, representatives,
   role quotas, and complete-treatment bound.
5. `widened-family-lane-retention`: carry every eligible existing-semantics family direction through
   field-conditional role evaluation; preserve control field-hypothesis, representative, role, and
   complete-treatment bounds.
6. `diagnostic-joint-availability-matrix`: report every registered field-direction by registered
   role-direction cell and its first disappearance stage. This arm changes no availability and is not
   selection-eligible.
7. `factorized-pareto-3000`: apply factorized Pareto retention with a 3,000-key logical-domain checkpoint,
   only after arms 2 through 5 and the matrix are complete.

`factorized-pareto-6000` may run only when `factorized-pareto-3000` fails the three-of-28 gate and its
deterministic custody trace proves that an otherwise qualifying lineage first disappears solely because
the exact 3,000-key checkpoint truncates it. A low recall count without that exact truncation proof does
not trigger 6,000. If triggered, 6,000 occupies the same final mechanism position; it is not another
mechanism or a combined arm.

Factorized checkpoints count unique deterministic canonical complete-treatment keys in logical
enumeration order before Pareto retention. They do not count only the retained Pareto set or only
materialized candidates. The enumeration rule and implementation ID must be frozen before execution.

## Bounds And Additivity

The materialized complete-candidate cap remains 1,500 per source and the public slate remains at most
eight. Every non-control arm must preserve every control materialized candidate and canonical legal key,
the control quality incumbent, every control identity obligation, and all `0.7.2` slate reservations and
guards. New candidates are additions only; no addition may compensate for lost control availability.

Control candidates occupy capacity first. An arm may materialize a new canonical key only in remaining
capacity under its predeclared deterministic order. If a source already has 1,500 control materialized
candidates, the arm materializes no addition for that source rather than evicting, replacing, or rewriting
a control candidate. Logical-only keys may remain diagnostic but cannot qualify for recall.

## Recall And Selection

A materialized treatment qualifies only when:

- its canonical key is absent from the control;
- every family, field, representative, and role edge has source-connected registry lineage;
- it is legal under unchanged cardinality, observability, and field-structure rules;
- it enters the unchanged ordinary Pareto frontier or passes the unchanged 12-block guard against that
  source's control quality incumbent; and
- it fills a registered field-direction and role-direction cell with no legal control treatment.

An independent selection-eligible arm passes at three distinct qualifying sources out of the fixed 28.
Aliases and multiple treatments from one source count once. The denominator never changes. The
qualifying-source count is the primary within-arm metric; after equal counts, same-arm implementations
compare fewer added unique canonical keys, lower mean runtime, then ASCII implementation ID.

Select the first passing selection-eligible mechanism in the fixed order. Review labels, comments, color
names, source IDs, filenames, literal colors, and named cases cannot select an arm or implementation. A
combined mechanism requires a separately versioned protocol.

## Resources And Gate

Runtime is measured and is a mechanical gate under one numeric per-source wall-clock ceiling frozen in
the execution manifest before any non-control arm runs. This protocol intentionally does not set that
number. A control-only calibration may run first on the bound host and runtime with the frozen worker
count; calibration is diagnostic, cannot inspect an ablation, and is not an arm result. Freeze the numeric
ceiling after calibration, then execute the control and every authorized arm under the same binding.

Memory may be used freely. This protocol defines no memory or RSS collection schema, measurement
requirement, gate, arm-selection authority, implementation tie-break, or candidate-retention input.

Before any successor protocol, generic fixtures and the fixed panel must also prove arm isolation,
registry completeness, exact ten-stage custody, additive control preservation, the materialized and slate
bounds, unchanged quality and winner authority, exact factorized counts and trigger handling, runtime-bound
compliance, and byte-identical repeated output. Failure is a bounded audit result, not authority to weaken
the gate or proceed to review, Phase 5, promotion, persistence, or a full roster.
