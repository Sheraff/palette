Review authorization amendment 1 records the explicit user instruction `If review, give me a review` on 2026-07-25. Deterministic manifest generation, rendering, and structured human feedback are authorized only under this contract. ## Purpose

Collect direct, source-grouped supervision for the two components that failed `factorized-field-state-authority-0.1.0-development` without reopening the gradient model or collapsing all palette roles into one judgment:

1. ordered two-field source-family pair identity;
2. one-field versus two-field multiplicity.

The native exact-pair gradient decision is a fixed conditional component. It is not fit from these judgments.

This contract does not authorize complete-tuple inference, unrestricted palette ranking, reserve-root access, autonomous review, or promotion.

## Preconditions

- The complete `native-exact-pair-topology-transfer-0.1.0-development` artifact must retain pass status.
- Its development SHA-256 must be bound before implementation.
- All 37 exposed image sources must reconstruct deterministically under the snapshot barriers.
- The frozen `native-field-hypothesis-graph-0.1.0-development` artifact remains historical evidence and is not silently rewritten. Horrorwood and Meteora contamination is carried as an explicit erratum.
- A separate explicit authorization is required before any review manifest, rendered presentation, or feedback file is created.

Roots `10` through `14`, autonomous feedback, free-text comment colors, and filename-derived model features are forbidden.

## Source Unit

The independent unit is exact encoded-source SHA-256. Archive copies, aliases, repeated presentations, and exact semantic transfers never become independent groups.

Only sources already exposed in the 37-source image roster may enter the first batch. Selection is deterministic and label-blind. Diagnostic sources are fixed before feedback and excluded from fitting.

## Single-Factor Presentations

Every comparison must preserve source binding, foreground RGB and generated status, accent RGB and generated status, typography, crop, layout, and presentation geometry. Exactly one field factor may change.

### Ordered Pair Identity

- Both options are two-field, distinct-flat presentations.
- Each option uses a different ordered pair of uniquely linked native primary families.
- Gradient state is fixed off so topology cannot influence pair preference.
- Foreground and accent are identical across options.
- Generated field endpoints are forbidden.

### Field Multiplicity

- Both options share the same background family and exact background RGB.
- The collapsed option repeats the background as surface with gradient off.
- The two-field option adds one preselected, uniquely linked native surface family with gradient off.
- Foreground and accent are identical across options.
- The comparison is invalid if any non-field role or presentation parameter differs.

### Gradient Diagnostic

- Both options use the same exact directed background and surface RGB values.
- One option is distinct-flat and the other is gradient.
- The native exact-pair scorer and its decision are sealed before feedback.
- These rows evaluate transfer only; they never refit a coefficient or threshold.

## Sampling

- Build source-first, then choose at most one comparison of each type per source in a batch.
- Require at least 24 exact source groups for ordered-pair judgments and 24 for multiplicity judgments before fitting.
- Prefer complete native linkage, field-role eligibility, endpoint distinguishability, and rendered visibility.
- Use deterministic graph uncertainty and coverage strata, never a target label or named-case branch.
- Freeze option order before rendering and counterbalance it deterministically across source hashes.
- Hold all diagnostic source hashes out of fitting and report them only after the model and thresholds are sealed.

If the exposed roster cannot provide the required independent groups without duplicate tuples or ambiguous linkage, stop before review rather than opening reserve roots.

## Structured Feedback

Each row records only:

- source eligibility;
- `a-stronger`, `b-stronger`, `either-way`, `no-visible-difference`, or `uncertain`;
- bounded structured reasons relevant to source fidelity, field identity, collapse, and unnecessary gradient;
- exact source SHA-256 and manifest case binding.

Free-text comments may be retained for qualitative context but cannot become RGB values, labels, features, gates, or named-case patches.

## Evaluation

- Deduplicate exact semantic tuples before fitting.
- Group all fitting and evaluation by exact source SHA-256.
- Fit the frozen deterministic source-balanced L2 Bradley-Terry procedure separately for pair identity and multiplicity.
- Standardization is training-fold-local.
- Evaluate with leave-one-exact-source-group-out predictions.
- Report source-balanced accuracy, source-balanced mean log loss, comparison accuracy, coverage, class direction, and per-source predictions.
- The fixed gradient component is evaluated on new direct gradient rows without fitting.

## Gates

All gates must pass before a complete-tuple evidence plan can be considered:

1. at least 24 mapped, decisive, non-diagnostic source groups for ordered-pair fitting;
2. at least 24 mapped, decisive, non-diagnostic source groups for multiplicity fitting;
3. complete grouped prediction coverage for both fitted components;
4. pair source-balanced accuracy at least `0.65` and mean log loss below `log(2)`;
5. multiplicity source-balanced accuracy at least `0.65` and mean log loss below `log(2)`;
6. pair accuracy improves on the frozen `0.5757575757575758` factorized result;
7. multiplicity accuracy improves on both the frozen fitted result `0.5` and uncalibrated ordering `0.6428571428571429`;
8. fixed native exact-pair gradient accuracy is at least `0.75` on mapped new direct gradient rows, with no threshold change;
9. the structured Krafty unnecessary-gradient regression remains prevented;
10. every diagnostic remains sealed until model fitting and thresholds are complete;
11. no interpreted comment target, filename branch, reserve source, or exact duplicate enters fitting.

Passing authorizes only a complete-tuple technical and evidence plan. It does not authorize palette output, further human review, reserve access, or promotion.

## Stop Conditions

Stop if independent source coverage is insufficient, a comparison changes more than one factor, endpoint linkage is ambiguous, grouped metrics fail, the fixed gradient component regresses, diagnostics leak into fitting, or any gate requires post hoc tuning. Do not compensate by opening reserve roots, reinterpreting comments, or branching around named examples.
