# Region Graph 0.8.0 Stable Rollback

The complete immutable state is in `region-graph-0.8.0.json`.

Version 0.8 removes the rejected 0.7 multi-background distinct-surface rule. All role colors and gradient decisions for spatial, expressive, and quantized methods across all 37 corpus entries exactly match the accepted 0.6 results.

No additional pairwise review was requested because:

- The restored Disney palette is exactly the 0.6 candidate preferred in the 0.7 review.
- The 0.7 alternative was not shippable and selected a shadow of the same pink field rather than another major artwork background.
- The rollback changes no other palette relative to 0.6.
- Re-reviewing an identical candidate would add duplicate evidence rather than test a new hypothesis.

The stable decision is to accept unified pink background and surface for Disney under the current single-foreground, exact-source-color, and WCAG constraints. Future work should test a broader held-out corpus or a richer role model rather than introduce further Disney-specific scoring.

## Post-Gallery Audit

The all-corpus gallery exposed that 0.8 still presented several spatial palettes previously rejected or criticized during baseline review. Elephunk, Franz, Loups, Nobs, and Skap had unshippable role assignments; Birds of Prey, Green Day, and Horsley retained specific unresolved issues.

This audit reopens those general failure classes for 0.9. It does not invalidate the immutable 0.8 results or rewrite their review history.
