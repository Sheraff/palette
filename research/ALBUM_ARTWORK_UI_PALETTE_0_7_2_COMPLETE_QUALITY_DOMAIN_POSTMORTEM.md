treatments, and otherwise returns to the `0.6.0` quality baseline. The distinction between a successful

## Implemented Repair

Version `0.7.2` preserves the `0.7.1` source-connected identity-obligation graph, ordinary quality
incumbent, candidate construction, retention frontiers, public-slate reservations, and exact-overlay
lineage. It changes only the winner quality boundary and its observability.

The selective six-block guard was replaced by the source-independent set union of the existing ordinary
Pareto and deterministic quality-ordering blocks. The union is derived from the two implementation
constant lists rather than maintained as an independent fitted subset. Its 12 members are:

1. `treatmentFoundation`;
2. `fieldIdentity`;
3. `fieldFidelity`;
4. `fieldStructure`;
5. `accentFidelity`;
6. `accentUtility`;
7. `artworkIdentity`;
8. `foregroundUtility`;
9. `representativeness`;
10. `coherence`;
11. `economy`; and
12. `surfaceFidelity`.

Each comparison continues to use the existing `0.04` evidence level after subtracting generated penalty.
A strict identity challenger passes only when it is non-inferior to the ordinary quality incumbent on
every union block. Obligation coverage and source-evidence priority are compared only among passing
challengers. An identity-descended exact-overlay candidate uses the same complete-domain filter against
the original quality incumbent.

The result version is `album-artwork-first-principles-0.7.2`. Graph version is
`identity-obligation-graph-v3`. Winner diagnostics version is
`pareto-identity-winner-diagnostics-v3`, and guard version is
`complete-quality-domain-non-inferiority-v1`.

Every strict identity challenger now serializes all compared evidence levels and every exact resolved
loss. Exact current ordinary-frontier membership is also serialized. Overlay projections are deduplicated
against existing complete treatments, and overlay evaluation count, uniqueness, and loss reconciliation
are mechanically checked.

## Generic Verification

Generic fixtures prove that every one of the 12 union dimensions is independently noncompensatory. For
each dimension, one resolved loss rejects a challenger even when every other dimension has a large gain.
Generated-penalty adjustment remains a separate generic check.

The exact-overlay generic fixture invokes the same production complete-domain filtering helper used by
identity selection and overlay selection. It proves that a structurally legal identity-descended overlay
with a loss in a Pareto-only dimension is rejected before overlay ordering.

The implementation and evaluator also bind the frozen `0.7.1` implementation, scientific result,
summary, and aggregate hashes. This prevents a same-version or same-manifest replacement from becoming
the continuity authority.

## Architecture Conclusion

Version `0.7.1` correctly diagnosed that identity needed a noncompensatory quality boundary but declared an
incomplete boundary. Version `0.7.2` closes the comparison domain using dimensions already present in the
architecture. It introduces no reviewed-case threshold, weighted identity score, color preference, or new
candidate generator.

The repair restores the intended authority order:

1. construct source-supported complete treatments;
2. establish the ordinary global Pareto quality incumbent;
3. require strict identity improvement;
4. require complete-domain quality non-inferiority;
5. compare identity coverage and priority among passing challengers; and
6. apply the same guard to any identity-descended overlay.

The fixed-panel result also sharpens the next research question. Identity obligations are available and
retained, but no strict challenger survives the complete quality boundary. Winner policy can no longer
compensate for a missing or weak complete treatment. Future progress must improve candidate availability
or source-derived evidence, not relax the guard.

## Direction For 0.7.3

The earlier postmortem proposed multi-hue field structure, role-specific identity attribution, and joint
field-role availability. A separate implementation audit suggests that aggressive pruning may remove
useful families, field hypotheses, representatives, or role combinations before complete treatments are
jointly evaluated.

These are not competing first steps. Excessive-pruning research is the cheapest discriminating form of the
postmortem's joint field-role availability question. A bounded `0.7.3` cycle should first determine whether
the missing complete treatments are generated and pruned, never jointly constructed, or impossible under
the current single-pair field model.

### 0.7.3 Research Unit

Begin `0.7.3` as a candidate-recall and stage-custody audit. Preserve `0.7.2` discovery semantics, scores,
ordinary Pareto dimensions, deterministic ordering, complete-domain quality guard, obligation priority,
winner authority, and public-slate guarantees. Widen one availability boundary at a time without granting
new candidates unconditional winner authority.

Before any ablation, create a source-derived pre-pruning direction registry after coherent-support evidence
is available but before lane, obligation, field-hypothesis, representative, or complete-treatment caps.
The registry must record every eligible family direction, field-hypothesis root, and legal foreground or
distinct-accent role root without asserting that each direction deserves winner authority. Declared
identity obligations remain a traced subset, not the audit denominator.

Run these independent arms against one byte-identical `0.7.2` control:

1. retain every existing representative strategy while preserving the current field-representative pairing
   rule and every other cap;
2. enumerate all legal background/surface cross-pairs among the currently retained representatives while
   preserving the current representative-strategy and family caps;
3. widen only field-hypothesis retention until field-conditional role evaluation while preserving current
   family lanes, representatives, role quotas, and complete-treatment bound;
4. widen only family-lane retention until field-conditional role evaluation while preserving current
   field-hypothesis, representative, role, and complete-treatment bounds;
5. report a joint availability matrix between every registered field direction and every registered role
   direction, with declared identity obligations identified as a separate subset; and
6. evaluate factorized Pareto retention as a separate arm only after arms 1 through 4 are complete. Use a
   3,000 logical-domain checkpoint first and a 6,000 checkpoint only when the 3,000 arm fails the declared
   recall gate because a qualifying lineage is truncated at that bound.

Do not combine successful arms within the first `0.7.3` cycle. If more than one independent arm passes,
prefer the first passing arm in the fixed order above. This is the predeclared minimum-scope tie rule.

The registry and audit must identify the exact first stage where each source-supported direction
disappears:

- discovered family;
- lane retention;
- field-hypothesis proposal;
- field-hypothesis retention;
- representative pairing;
- field-conditional role eligibility;
- complete-treatment construction;
- ordinary Pareto membership;
- complete-domain guard; or
- public-slate retention.

### Why Recall Comes First

The known candidate-domain failure retained important role ingredients but did not expose one legal
complete treatment combining the desired field and role properties. That observation does not yet prove
whether the field model is insufficient or whether current-model ingredients were removed too early.

Adding multi-hue field structure before testing recall would add a larger hypothesis language, more
gradient false-positive risk, and a harder attribution problem. Conversely, simply making all search
exhaustive is not supported by existing evidence. An adjacent broad-search candidate was rated baseline
stronger 47 times and candidate stronger 14 times. This is bound by
`research/data/experiments/next-palette-0.1.0-development/review-v2/interim-disposition-batches-01-02.json`,
SHA-256 `d09fad90360e3ec4d949e91068e3cc99e8ecc430fca955903795c94a0a4d65ff`. A separate exhaustive native
search reached roughly 158 seconds and 1 GB RSS on its worst source while producing only two material
source-group changes. This is bound by
`research/data/experiments/native-complete-palette-0.2.5-development/phase-5/analysis.json`, SHA-256
`13e81f5f16099c42b62270c24c77ef555ad668f0752464e581282acb4361c453`.

Factorized search is a more credible scale mechanism. Prior diagnostic work represented about 531 million
logical complete tuples at roughly 3.18 seconds mean runtime per source. This is bound by
`research/data/experiments/joint-palette-evidence-frontier-0.1.1-development/analysis.json`, SHA-256
`7880923c77ffb152122ba1d543213395e3489f454ba4b66e84111a68d1224d2e`. One targeted reviewed subset
recorded seven candidate-stronger outcomes against two baseline-stronger outcomes, but it failed its
predeclared zero-regression gate and was not promotion-authorized. This mixed diagnostic evidence is bound
by
`research/data/experiments/joint-palette-ablation-stable-noncollapsed-field-0.1.0-development/review-v2/interpretation.json`,
SHA-256 `9c87721ab07798a1e5d7013d6563792e6635ea02c21b832bd0083335a47668be`.

These experiments are not direct V2 evidence and do not establish a successful factorized candidate. They
justify testing factorized retention instead of equating larger search with better quality.

### Deferred Mechanisms

Do not combine the first `0.7.3` recall audit with:

- smaller family bins or iterative family refinement;
- retaining all components before repetition and polarity inference;
- additional gradient angles or movable radial centers;
- denser gradient grids or contrast sampling;
- piecewise or multi-hue field hypotheses;
- new role-semantic scores; or
- a higher winner-authority score.

Those mechanisms change discovery or evidence semantics and would prevent a clean conclusion about
post-discovery pruning. They remain justified follow-up questions if the bounded recall audit does not
recover treatments that satisfy the recall gate below.

### 0.7.3 Recall Gate Definition

A newly available treatment qualifies for the recall gate only when all of these clauses hold:

- its exact complete-treatment key is absent from the byte-identical control arm;
- every family, field, representative, and role edge has source-connected registry lineage;
- it is legal under unchanged cardinality, observability, and field-structure rules;
- it either enters the unchanged ordinary Pareto frontier or passes the unchanged 12-block complete-domain
  guard against the source's `0.7.2` quality incumbent; and
- it fills a field-direction and role-direction joint-availability cell that has no legal treatment in the
  control arm.

An independent ablation arm passes candidate recall only when at least three distinct sources in the fixed
28-source panel each gain at least one qualifying treatment. Three is the predeclared minimum source count;
the denominator is always 28, and aliases or multiple treatments from one source count once. The arm must
also preserve every control quality incumbent, identity obligation, existing legal treatment key, and
public-slate bound. Candidate additions may not be offset by a loss of control availability.

The arm's qualifying source count is its primary recall metric and determines whether it passes the fixed
threshold. It does not override the fixed mechanism-scope order across different boundaries. If two
implementations of the same arm would otherwise tie, compare fewer added unique complete treatments, then
lower mean runtime, then lower maximum RSS, then ASCII implementation ID. No review result may choose among
arms or implementations.

### 0.7.3 Evidence Gate

Before any human review, the fixed development panel and generic fixtures must show:

- the control arm is byte-identical to frozen `0.7.2`;
- each ablation changes only its declared retention or construction boundary;
- all new candidate lineage is source-connected and stage-exact;
- candidate, logical-domain, runtime, and memory bounds are predeclared;
- every current quality incumbent remains retained;
- every identity-selected treatment still passes the 12-block complete-domain guard;
- every newly available treatment reports whether it enters the ordinary frontier, is complete-domain
  non-inferior, and jointly carries a registered field direction plus registered role direction, with
  declared identity-obligation coverage reported separately;
- each passing arm satisfies the exact three-of-28 recall gate above;
- repeated execution is byte-identical; and
- no review label, comment, filename, source ID, literal color, or named-case branch enters inference.

Generic fixtures should cover broad two-stage transitions, interrupted transitions, hard flat regions,
object-local ramps, unrelated colorful details, dark and light typography, non-text dark objects, repeated
decorative marks, and polarity reversals. These fixtures diagnose availability stages; they do not encode a
desired reviewed output.

### 0.7.3 Decision Boundary

If an independent arm passes the exact recall gate, `0.7.3` may select one mechanism using the fixed scope
and tie rules above. A combined mechanism requires another separately versioned cycle. Any human delta
must remain bounded to genuinely novel treatments and may begin only after the selected arm independently
passes all mechanical gates.

If the widened current-model search still cannot construct the missing field-role combinations, excessive
pruning is falsified as the primary cause. The next research unit should then move to multi-hue field
structure with separately predeclared hypotheses and generic fixtures.

This decision ends the `0.7.3` research unit. Multi-hue fields, role-specific attribution, and any combined
field-role construction belong to the roadmap after `0.7.3`, below.

## Research After 0.7.3

The limited `0.7.3` unit does not consume or replace the remaining architecture questions from the `0.7.1`
postmortem. It isolates current-model candidate recall first. The following research remains separately
versioned and separately gated.

### Multi-Hue Field Structure

If widened current-model recall still cannot construct important field-role combinations, investigate
piecewise or multi-hue field hypotheses across multiple broad connected regions. The mechanism must derive
endpoint families, sequence, and topology from source geometry and progression. It may not construct a
reviewed hue request from named colors or a case-specific branch.

The first generic fixture set should include broad two-stage chromatic transitions, interrupted
transitions, hard flat regions, object-local ramps, fragmented details, and unrelated colorful objects.
The mechanism must distinguish a major source field progression from stripes, local lighting, and arbitrary
palette-color pairs. It requires its own architecture version, candidate bounds, field-hypothesis lineage,
and fixed-panel comparison against `0.7.2` or the accepted `0.7.3` mechanism.

### Role-Specific Identity Attribution

After field availability is established, investigate whether source-connected identity families can be
attributed specifically to foreground-like or accent-like roles. Use source-derived geometry, repetition,
polarity, component structure, local contrast, and observability evidence rather than a preference for dark
or light colors.

Generic fixtures must include dark and light typography, polarity reversals, non-text dark and light
objects, repeated decorative marks, isolated details, and signature accents. This research may not encode a
literal foreground color, typography comment, named artwork, or reviewed role assignment.

### Mechanism-Aware Joint Construction

Current-model joint availability is part of `0.7.3`. A later audit is still required after multi-hue field
or role-specific attribution mechanisms exist. It must report whether every defensible new field hypothesis
can coexist with every defensible role-specific identity direction in a legal complete treatment.

That audit must separate failure at field discovery, role attribution, representative availability,
complete construction, ordinary Pareto ordering, complete-domain guard, and slate retention. Retention may
preserve joint field-role representatives, but joint availability does not grant unconditional winner
authority.

### Discovery-Semantic Follow-Ups

Only after the post-discovery recall result is known should later work consider smaller family bins,
iterative family refinement, retaining more components before repetition evidence, additional gradient
angles or centers, denser fitting grids, or denser contrast sampling. Each changes evidence semantics and
therefore requires its own one-factor protocol and generic controls.

### Post-0.7.3 Advancement Boundary

Do not combine multi-hue fields, role-specific attribution, joint construction, and discovery-semantic
changes in one candidate. Advance one mechanism only after it shows source-independent value across
multiple development sources, preserves the complete-domain guard, and produces a genuinely novel bounded
human delta. No directional sample, Phase 5, promotion, persistence, or full-roster execution is authorized
by this roadmap.

## Advancement Boundary

Version `0.7.2` becomes the development architecture and safety baseline for `0.7.3`. Version `0.6.0`
remains the product and promotion comparison baseline until a separate decision explicitly promotes a
successor.

No directional sample should be opened merely because `0.7.2` is mechanically successful. A future
candidate must first prove broad, source-independent development value under the complete-domain guard.
No Phase 5, persistence, full-roster execution, or production replacement follows from this postmortem.
