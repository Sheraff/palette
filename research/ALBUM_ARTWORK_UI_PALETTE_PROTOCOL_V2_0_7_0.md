# Album Artwork UI Palette Candidate Protocol V2 0.7.0

Status: bounded development identity-obligation architecture. Normative product direction remains
`research/ALBUM_ARTWORK_UI_PALETTE_PLAN_V2.md`. Candidate
`album-artwork-first-principles-0.6.0` is an immutable predecessor and is not eligible for promotion.

This protocol does not authorize a directional sample, Phase 5, promotion, persistence, full-roster
execution, or a multi-hour run. Consumed directional samples and their colors, case IDs, outputs,
comments, and technical interpretations may not become implementation inputs, fixtures, expected
outputs, thresholds, or named branches.

## Purpose

Identity-bearing evidence can be lost independently when role options are bounded, complete treatments
are constructed, a global Pareto frontier is retained, and top-one is selected. Version `0.7.0`
therefore replaces post-hoc identity compensation with an explicit identity-obligation graph. The graph
must preserve inspectable source-to-winner custody for general source-connected, materially distinct
signature evidence.

Existing native evidence, field inference, representative support, role legality, APCA observability,
complete-treatment scoring, and global Pareto calculations remain available. The identity graph is a
new structural retention and selection layer, not an additive score, fitted threshold, or source-specific
exception.

## Obligation Roots

- Start from the existing bounded signature lane.
- A signature family is field-owned rather than an identity obligation when its `0.04` field-evidence
  level is at least its signature-role evidence level.
- A remaining family must contain a role-observed connected component with more than one source pixel.
  A one-pixel color cannot create an obligation.
- Its prototype must be at least `0.025` OKLab from field-owned evidence and from every higher-priority
  retained obligation. This is also the existing visual-nearness boundary used by slate diversity.
- Rank eligible roots deterministically by connected-region evidence level, signature-role evidence
  level, connected population, then ASCII family ID.
- Retain at most four obligations. Serialize field-owned, disconnected, insufficiently distinct,
  redundant-direction, and bound-omitted family IDs so obligation selection itself remains inspectable.
- No filename, source ID, review label, comment, color name, target color, or directional-sample datum may
  enter obligation discovery or ordering.

## Graph Stages

Each retained obligation has exactly these nodes and consecutive edges:

1. `source-signature` records source-connected, materially distinct signature support.
2. `role-availability` records whether a legal peak-observable foreground or distinct accent option
   exists.
3. `complete-treatment` records complete treatments carrying the obligation as foreground or distinct
   accent.
4. `retention-frontier` records identity-stratified retention in addition to the unchanged global Pareto
   frontier.
5. `retained-slate` records the reserved complete alternative carrying the obligation.
6. `winner-explanation` records whether top-one covers the obligation, defers it to a retained
   alternative, or could not receive it because an earlier stage was blocked.

Every node includes status, reason, applicable roles, treatment count, and up to eight deterministic
treatment IDs. Every edge records whether the obligation was carried to its destination. Runtime
invariants fail extraction if an available obligation lacks a complete treatment, a complete obligation
lacks a retention-frontier representative, or a retained obligation lacks a slate representative.

## Availability And Construction

- Obligation family IDs receive deterministic reservation before ordinary family directions fill the
  existing foreground and per-foreground accent quotas.
- The ordinary APCA peak-observability, exact role equality, source support, representative, contrast,
  and cardinality rules remain mandatory. An obligation does not legalize an otherwise invalid role.
- At most four obligations fit within the existing minimum of six foreground directions and four
  distinct accents per foreground. Candidate generation remains bounded at 1,500.
- A treatment covers an obligation only when the obligation family is its foreground or its distinct
  accent. A collapsed accent does not claim a second identity direction.

## Frontier, Slate, And Winner

- Compute the unchanged evidence-level global Pareto frontier and report its counts separately.
- Add the best within-obligation Pareto representative for every feasible obligation to a retention
  frontier. Also retain the best complete treatment under the identity ordering so combined obligation
  coverage cannot be discarded by per-obligation stratification.
- Reserve the winner and one representative for each still-uncovered feasible obligation before ordinary
  field-kind, field-direction, and complete-direction diversity fills the eight-treatment slate.
- Top-one first maximizes the number of obligations covered by one complete treatment, then compares
  obligation coverage in source-evidence priority order, then applies the existing deterministic
  complete-treatment priority blocks and ASCII tie-breaker.
- Identity coverage is lexicographic structural policy. It is not added to `artworkIdentity`, any score,
  Pareto dominance, or a weighted sum.
- The exact-overlay gradient challenger may only replace this primary winner while preserving its exact
  foreground and accent colors and family IDs. It therefore cannot reduce identity-obligation coverage.

## Diagnostics And Bounds

- Result version is `album-artwork-first-principles-0.7.0`.
- Graph version is `identity-obligation-graph-v1`.
- At most four obligations, 24 graph nodes, 20 graph edges, 1,500 complete candidates, and eight retained
  treatments are allowed.
- Global Pareto candidate and direction counts remain distinct from retention-frontier count and the
  number of identity-carried non-global candidates.
- Winner diagnostics bind the selection reason, feasible, covered, and deferred obligation IDs, and the
  maximum obligation coverage available in the complete domain.
- Extraction remains deterministic and metadata-independent.

## Development Gate

Use only the fixed authorized development panel and source-independent synthetic fixtures. Mechanical
development must show:

- isolated one-pixel noise never creates an obligation;
- every root has connected source regions and the declared material separation;
- every satisfied availability node is satisfied at complete construction, retention frontier, and
  retained slate;
- winner coverage equals maximum feasible complete-treatment coverage;
- blocked and deferred obligations have explicit graph explanations;
- ordinary palette legality and candidate/slate bounds continue to hold;
- repeated extraction is byte-identical; and
- no consumed or unopened directional sample is executed or converted into an implementation target.

Human evaluation, candidate freezing, and selection of any new source-independent directional sample
require separate protocols after broad development evidence supports this architecture.
