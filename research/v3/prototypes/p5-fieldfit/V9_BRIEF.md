## The mechanism

A **mark/region support instrument**: group the image (overlay AND field-unexplained texture)
into spatially connected components at a scale chosen by scale-space stability (arm-f §2.3:
sweep grouping scales, take the plateau where component count is flattest — the criterion is the
human decision, not a constant; range tied to the image diagonal). Each mark/region carries:
connected support, spatially-accumulated mass (every pixel counts — no triple floor inside a
mark), robust colour, chroma, ink-likeness (mortality/adjacency NOW measurable on real connected
support — this un-defers decision 15b), and its local field.

Three consumers, in priority order:
1. **Identity families v2**: the identity set is read off marks/regions by spatially-accumulated
   mass, not off surviving triples. Anchor: the NARCOSIS crimson must appear in the top-4
   identity set (m1 has its exact numbers). The set-coverage machinery (decision 18) then runs
   unchanged over real families.
2. **Accent candidacy**: marks/regions join the accent pool with their spatial mass (the union
   principle extends downward from components). Anchor: NARCOSIS publishes the crimson family as
   accent; the sunset STRONG and all round-4 silent STRONGs stay byte-identical.
3. **Foreground ink preference (15b un-deferred)**: among floor-clearing candidates, ink-shaped
   marks lead. Anchors: round-3 items 4 (white title) and 8 (black type) finally answered;
   round-4 item 4's STRONG fg (raw 16.93) must hold — ink preference must not displace it
   (measure: is `#201c13` ink-shaped there? If the preference would displace a STRONG, the
   preference is ordered BELOW holding class — bring the measurement, not a guess).

## Constraints

All standing SPEC decisions bind. The grouping is connectivity-of-the-fit's-rejections plus
texture, never a segmentation-first pass on the raster (the paradigm line holds: fit first, group
the residual). Constants: the scale-sweep criterion per arm-f (rule, not value); everything else
provenance-tagged. Determinism throughout; ties end at packed int. Cost: the instrument must not
regress the 497 s / 600-trial robustness envelope by more than ~2× (measure; the quickselect
lesson says profile before optimizing).
