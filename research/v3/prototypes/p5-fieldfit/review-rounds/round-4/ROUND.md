## What we do differently per outcome

- **(1) graded 1–2** → the three-round rotation closes; assignment stack (decisions 15–18)
  validated end-to-end. **3+** → the set/class work failed its motivating cover; mechanism
  re-opens at the assignment layer, not tuning.
- **(2) regresses (3+ or an accent note)** → the class term extends to the accent, verdict as
  provenance; **holds (1–2, silent)** → the union stands and incumbent-anchoring stays rejected.
- **(3) graded 1–2** → veto validated; a residual "many colors" note at grade ≤2 downgrades the
  item-6 class gap to a ceiling issue. **Still 4** → the class needs its own mechanism round.
- **(4) graded 1–2 with no accent note** → named prediction confirmed on the third try. A note
  naming a *different* family → the accent criterion is still wrong in a way three rounds
  haven't localized; escalates to a mechanism question with all three data points.
- **(5) fg complaint about light ink** → the legibility-floor bracket (10.6, 28.9] gets its
  light-field evidence; **clean** → the floor generalizes.
- **(6) UNACCEPTABLE again** → item-6 class is structural (mark-level grouping moves from v0.9
  queue to next); **improved** → one-cover finding, queue unchanged.
