## What we do differently per outcome

- **(1) graded 1–2, no accent note** → the accent line closes (named ask delivered, three rounds
  of provenance); an accent note naming yet another colour → the line escalates to the reviewer
  as a criterion question with the full history, because three rule generations will then have
  failed the same cover class.
- **(2) silent** → coherence gate validated, nothing further; **any note** → the gate's radius or
  the family union needs its second look with the note as evidence.
- **(3) grade improves** → identity direction pays even without semantics; the face-as-field axis
  stays open and honestly unowned. **Grade flat with the semantic note recurring** → the semantic
  axis becomes the top open question for the reviewer's mechanism-selection read (it is the one
  thing this arm cannot express); record, do not build.
- **(4) hits the class and grades ≤2** → the class was one bad draw, closed; **another miss or a
  4** → the class is recorded as un-censusable by current statistics and goes to the reviewer as
  a known hole in this arm's draw protocol.
- **(5–8) at parity with their classes' history** → v0.9 generalizes; **systematically worse** →
  the identity cycle overfit its four driving covers and the cycle re-opens on the failing class.
