   re-ranking (min |APCA| over the ramp's stop pixels). Keep; W6's attribution proceeds unchanged.
2. **Indistinguishable sibling pairs are forbidden — collapse instead of publishing twins.** Our
   collapse machinery triggers at the same-colour bar; the reviewer's "indistinguishable" may be
   wider. Do not move the bar on principle (numbers don't bind); instead: round-1 notes are the
   calibration instrument — watch for twin complaints on pairs that cleared the bar. Diagnostic to
   add in the next iteration: publish-time sibling distances (bg↔surface, fg↔accent) in the
   exposed intermediates so twin-risk is countable per run.
3. **Palettes must carry artwork identity** (vivid accents mined from the artwork; only shades

   departure, the ink regime publishes the designer's ink. The discipline line stays pure; any
   future tuning that would trade exactness away is now evidence-contradicted, not just
   line-forbidden.
4. **Gradient on a flat artwork is a graded-down error.** ρ* = 0.6 is [UNCALIBRATED] and demo-20
   published gradients on 8/20 — rate unvalidated. Round 1 carries 2 gradient items; their grades
   + notes are the first ρ* calibration evidence. If a false gradient is flagged, ρ* calibration
