## Interpretation

The selected availability repair is product-visible. It changes top-one on two sources and supplies many
novel slate alternatives under the unchanged selector. The result therefore rejects the
`no-product-visible-delta` branch.

The evidence does not yet distinguish a useful candidate from a weak or regressive candidate. Both changed
winners lose three resolved quality blocks against their prior `0.7.2` incumbents, while 44 novel alternatives
reach slates without top-one. Human complete-treatment review is needed to determine whether:

- the two changed winners are acceptable or strong despite the diagnostic losses;
- the broad novel slate delta contains visibly useful alternatives;
- the unchanged selector is misordering strong alternatives; or
- the recalled treatments remain technically valid but visually weak.

No ranking change follows mechanically. Deterministic-ranking research remains a later, separately versioned
unit only if authorized review repeatedly shows strong retained alternatives and weaker top-one selection.
