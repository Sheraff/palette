## Quality Incumbent

The quality incumbent is the first treatment under the existing ordinary global Pareto frontier and
deterministic quality ordering. It is computed before identity winner selection and supplemental exact
overlay projection. Its exact treatment must always be retained in the public slate, including when it
shares a complete direction with an identity carrier.

## Identity Challenger

A treatment is an identity challenger only when its obligation vector is strictly better than the
quality incumbent's vector: first by covered-obligation count, then by obligation presence in the
pre-existing source-evidence priority order. Equal vectors do not create a challenger.

Every challenger is compared independently with the quality incumbent in these six blocks:

1. `treatmentFoundation`;
2. `fieldIdentity`;
3. `foregroundUtility`;
4. `accentUtility`;
5. `coherence`; and
6. `economy`.

These blocks are necessary as a noncompensatory boundary around complete field structure, field identity,
both active-role utilities, cross-role coherence, and role economy. Removing one would permit identity to
reintroduce a reviewed architecture failure class through that unguarded dimension.

Each comparison uses the existing `0.04` absolute evidence level after subtracting the treatment's
generated penalty. A challenger passes only when its evidence level is at least the incumbent's in all
six blocks. A gain in any block cannot compensate for a resolved loss in another. No weighted identity
score or new fitted threshold is introduced.

Among passing challengers, select maximum obligation coverage, then obligation priority, then the existing
deterministic treatment-quality ordering and ASCII tie-breaker. If no strictly better challenger passes,
retain the quality incumbent as primary.

## Gradient Lineage

The existing exact-overlay gradient challenger remains supplemental and must preserve the primary's exact
foreground and accent colors and family IDs. When identity selected the primary, an exact-overlay
replacement must also pass all six quality blocks against the original quality incumbent. This prevents
projection from bypassing the identity quality guard.
