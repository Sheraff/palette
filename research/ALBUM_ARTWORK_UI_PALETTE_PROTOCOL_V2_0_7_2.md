## Purpose

Version `0.7.1` preserved source-to-slate identity custody and restored the ordinary global Pareto top as
the quality incumbent, but its six-block guard omitted dimensions already used by ordinary Pareto or the
deterministic quality ordering. Version `0.7.2` preserves the graph and candidate domain while closing the
winner comparison over the union of those existing dimensions.

## Preserved Domain

The `0.7.1` rules for source-connected obligation discovery, material separation, role reservation,
complete-treatment closure, obligation-stratified frontier retention, slate reservation, and six-stage
explanation remain unchanged. The limits remain four obligations, 1,500 complete candidates, six nodes
and five edges per obligation, six exact-overlay projections, and eight public treatments.

Field discovery, field hypotheses, gradient eligibility, role attribution, complete-treatment generation,
ordinary Pareto dominance, deterministic quality ordering, and identity priority order are unchanged.
Every feasible identity obligation remains represented in the public slate whether or not its carriers
can pass the winner guard.

## Complete Quality Domain

The ordinary Pareto block list and deterministic quality-ordering block list remain independent
implementation constants. The complete guard block list is derived as their source-independent set union,
without a third fitted score or manually maintained selective subset.

The current union is:

1. `treatmentFoundation`;
2. `fieldIdentity`;
3. `fieldFidelity`;
4. `fieldStructure`;
5. `accentFidelity`;
6. `accentUtility`;
7. `artworkIdentity`;
8. `foregroundUtility`;
9. `representativeness`;
10. `coherence`;
11. `economy`; and
12. `surfaceFidelity`.

The serialized order follows the deterministic ordering first and appends dimensions unique to ordinary
Pareto. Membership, not list position, defines the conjunctive quality boundary.

Each comparison uses the existing `0.04` absolute evidence level after subtracting the treatment's
generated penalty. A challenger passes only when its evidence level is at least the quality incumbent's
in every union block. A gain in any block cannot compensate for a resolved loss in another. No new score,
weight, threshold, or candidate is introduced.

## Winner Selection

The quality incumbent remains the first treatment under the existing ordinary global Pareto frontier and
deterministic quality ordering. Its exact treatment must remain in the public slate.

A treatment is an identity challenger only when its obligation vector is strictly better than the
incumbent's vector: first by covered-obligation count, then by obligation presence in the existing
source-evidence priority order. Every strict challenger is evaluated against the incumbent over the
complete quality domain before obligation coverage and priority select among passing challengers. If no
strict challenger passes, the incumbent remains primary.

An exact-overlay gradient descendant of an identity-selected primary must pass the same complete-domain
guard against the original quality incumbent. Incumbent-descended overlay behavior is unchanged.

## Diagnostics

Result version is `album-artwork-first-principles-0.7.2`. Graph version is
`identity-obligation-graph-v3`. Winner diagnostics version is
`pareto-identity-winner-diagnostics-v3`, and guard version is
`complete-quality-domain-non-inferiority-v1`.

Winner diagnostics serialize the ordinary Pareto blocks, deterministic ordering blocks, and derived guard
union separately. Every strict identity challenger and every guard-compared exact-overlay descendant binds:

- incumbent and challenger treatment IDs;
- every compared block and both exact evidence levels;
- the conjunctive pass result; and
- every resolved-loss block, both levels, and exact level difference.

Rejected-challenger loss lists must equal all and only failed block rows. Aggregate counts may not replace
these per-challenger records.

## Development Gate

Before any human delta, generic fixtures and the fixed 28-source development panel must prove:

- every union dimension is independently noncompensatory;
- all `0.7.1` discovery, availability, construction, retention, graph, and slate guarantees remain;
- candidate and slate bounds remain unchanged;
- the ordinary quality incumbent is always retained;
- every selected identity challenger and identity-descended overlay passes every union block;
- every rejected challenger identifies all exact resolved losses;
- repeated extraction is byte-identical;
- pre-winner evidence and ordinary frontiers are continuous with frozen `0.7.1`; and
- every winner change is attributable only to the complete-domain guard.

The resulting winners must be compared with frozen `0.7.1`, failed `0.7.0`, and quality baseline `0.6.0`.
An earlier human assessment may transfer only when both source SHA-256 and complete-treatment key match.
Only genuinely novel treatments may enter a new blinded bounded delta. No directional sample is opened by
this protocol.
