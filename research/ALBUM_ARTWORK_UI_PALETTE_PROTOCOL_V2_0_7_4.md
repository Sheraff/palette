- `0.7.4` receives a new implementation closure and implementation hash. It may not overwrite or relabel
  any `0.7.2` or `0.7.3` artifact.

## Candidate Construction

For each supplied development source, construct both domains from current source pixels and the same
bound implementation closure. Historical palette output is comparison evidence only and is never loaded by
the extractor.

1. Run the exact `0.7.2` native evidence, family lanes, field domains, gradient fitting, representative
   construction, role evidence, collapse, contrast, legality, score, and control generation path.
2. Preserve that complete control domain as the deterministic prefix. Do not evict, replace, mutate, or
   reorder a control treatment.
3. Carry every eligible existing-semantics field-hypothesis proposal through field-conditional role
   evaluation. This is the only changed availability boundary.
4. Use unchanged preferred representatives, two-representative role cap, same-index field pairing,
   field-conditional foreground quota, four-distinct-accent quota, emergency-color rule, treatment
   constructor, and canonical treatment key.
5. Reject an addition before capacity if any family, hypothesis, field, representative, or role edge lacks
   registered source-connected lineage. Add each novel canonical key once in frozen deterministic order
   using only capacity remaining after the control prefix.
6. Apply the shared unchanged ordinary Pareto, deterministic quality order, ordinary-incumbent, identity,
   12-block complete-domain guard, exact-overlay, winner, and diverse-slate implementations to the complete
   additive domain.
7. Emit one normal `AlbumArtworkPaletteV2Result`, including its recomputed winner and at most eight retained
   treatments. The `0.7.3` audit slate is not the candidate output.

The canonical key is lower-case canonical sRGB in role order:
`#rrggbb:#rrggbb:#rrggbb:#rrggbb:gradient` or
`#rrggbb:#rrggbb:#rrggbb:#rrggbb:flat`.

## Preserved Policy

All policy other than field-hypothesis retention is exact `0.7.2` policy:

- native-resolution discovery; tolerant families; field, signature, and foreground lanes;
- one-field, separate-flat-field, and single-pair gradient language;
- canonical `srgb-uint8` output and lower-case six-digit display hex;
- only `surface === background` and `accent === foreground` collapse; background and foreground are
  distinct; no other equality is legal; two to four distinct colors;
- `gradient: true` requires a distinct surface and a collapsed surface requires `gradient: false`;
- 135-degree linear OKLab gradient from positions 0 to 1, sampled for contrast at
  `0`, `0.25`, `0.5`, `0.75`, and `1`;
- APCA W3 `0.1.9` signed Lc, zero research hard floor, and unchanged observability rules;
- at most one emergency pure black or white, considered only when every source-supported
  background/foreground pair has absolute Lc at most `5`, with generated-color penalty `0.18`;
- maximum synthesized occupied distance `0.025`, identity material distance `0.025`, evidence resolution
  `0.04`, and no cardinality reward;
- family lanes `12/16/16`, two representatives per role, same-index field pairing, a computed foreground
  quota of at least six per field variant, four distinct accents per foreground, and at most four identity
  obligations;
- complete-candidate cap `1,500`, exact-overlay projection cap `6`, and public-slate cap `8`;
- ordinary Pareto blocks, deterministic ranking blocks, 12-block complete-quality guard, identity
  obligation graph v3, winner diagnostics v3, and exact-overlay authority; and
- deterministic metadata-independent ordering with no randomness.

All-strategy representative retention, field-representative cross-pairing, family-lane widening,
factorized retention, additional gradient forms, multi-hue fields, role-specific identity attribution,
new scores or thresholds, incumbent fallback, cross-version inference veto, and learned ranking are false.

## Closure And Runtime

Before calibration, freeze a candidate implementation closure containing the V2 plan, `0.7.3` handoff,
this protocol and constants module, all transitive candidate source files, evaluator and child, independent
analyzer, focused generic and artifact tests, fixed panel, protected-seal bindings, package manifest,
lockfile, Node version, platform, architecture, and command lines. Every file receives path, byte count, and
raw SHA-256 in the `0.7.4` execution manifest. The implementation hash is derived from that complete ordered
binding, not copied from `0.7.3`.

Runtime protocol is exact:

- primary panel workers: `6`; scheduling-invariance workers: `1`;
- run control-only calibration before any candidate extraction under both worker configurations;
- predeclare the candidate per-source per-run wall ceiling as
  `max(60000, ceil(6 * maximumControlCalibrationWallMs / 1000) * 1000)` milliseconds;
- publish the numeric ceiling, calibration measurements, runtime identity, worker counts, dispatch orders,
  and implementation hash immutably before the first candidate run;
- execute candidate pass A with one worker and ascending case ID, then candidate pass B with six workers
  and descending dispatch order; canonical aggregate order remains ascending case ID;
- record wall time for every source in both passes and fail if either measurement exceeds the frozen
  ceiling; and
- require byte-identical extraction/scientific payloads and equal aggregate scientific output across both
  passes. Runtime and scheduling metadata are outside scientific identity.

Memory may be used freely. No memory, RSS, heap, or resident-set field, collection requirement, ceiling,
gate, retention input, disposition authority, or tie-break is defined. Incidental operating-system memory
data has no protocol authority.

## Mechanical Gates

All clauses are conjunctive:

- every direction, handoff, panel, control, product-baseline, `0.7.3`, and protected-seal binding verifies;
- the frozen 28-source denominator and exact source hashes match; no protected source is accessed;
- the live `0.7.4` control extraction is byte-identical to each immutable `0.7.2` source extraction;
- `field-hypothesis-retention` is the only changed stage and every addition descends from an eligible
  existing-semantics proposal absent only because of control retention;
- every control canonical key and exact treatment remains an ordered prefix, and every control quality
  incumbent, identity obligation, and required slate reservation remains available;
- every addition has complete registered source-connected lineage and unchanged legality, cardinality,
  observability, field structure, representative, contrast, generated-color, and score semantics;
- materialized complete candidates are at most `1,500`, exact-overlay projections at most `6`, and public
  treatments at most `8` per source;
- treatment construction, ordinary Pareto, ordering, identity, guard, overlay, winner, and slate stages are
  the same implementations for control and candidate domains;
- every identity-selected treatment and identity-descended overlay passes all 12 guard blocks against the
  candidate run's ordinary quality incumbent;
- both candidate passes are byte-identical scientifically, worker count and dispatch order have no effect,
  canonical panel ordering is stable, and every measured wall time passes the predeclared ceiling;
- all compact artifacts reconcile under the independent analyzer and final manifest; and
- inference contains no prior palette, baseline fallback, review label, comment, interpretation, color name,
  filename, path, source ID, literal target color, named-case branch, randomness, or worker-schedule input.

Any failed clause selects `mechanical-closure-failed` and blocks all review or advancement.

1. `mechanical-closure-failed`: any mechanical clause fails.
2. `novel-final-winner-delta-awaiting-separate-review-authorization`: closure passes and at least one novel

ranking defect, opens a review, or changes a baseline. Any bounded development review or diagnostic review
