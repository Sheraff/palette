ordinary Pareto blocks, deterministic ordering, complete-quality-domain guard, identity obligations, winner
authority, exact-overlay behavior, and public-slate bounds.

It adds:

- an instrumented control whose extraction is byte-identical to every frozen `0.7.2` source artifact;
- a canonical complete-treatment key and complete control treatment domain;
- a pre-pruning source direction registry;
- a field-direction by role-direction availability matrix;

Memory and RSS are not collected and have no gate, retention, or selection authority. Control-only runtime

## Selection

The recall gate required at least three distinct qualifying sources out of 28. Three mechanisms passed, but
the predeclared fixed order overrides later-arm counts and runtime. The first passing mechanism is
`widened-field-hypothesis-retention`, so it is the sole selected mechanism.

The family-lane and factorized mechanisms remain diagnostic evidence. They may not be combined with the
selected mechanism in this cycle, and their results do not authorize an alternative selection.

The representative and cross-pair arms are useful negative results. They generated many legal additions but
none both filled a control-empty field-role cell and entered the ordinary frontier or passed the complete
quality boundary. Representative-strategy and same-domain field-pair pruning are not the primary recall
failure exposed by this panel.

## Architecture Conclusion

The current single-pair field language can construct source-connected, legal, ordinary-frontier treatments
that the `0.7.2` field-hypothesis cap removes before field-conditional role evaluation. Candidate availability,
not winner-policy relaxation, is the demonstrated next intervention point.

The selected mechanism must remain narrow:

- carry all existing-semantics field-hypothesis proposals through field-conditional role evaluation;
- preserve current family lanes, representative policy, role quotas, scoring, Pareto dimensions, complete
  quality guard, winner authority, identity obligations, and slate guarantees;
- preserve every control treatment and incumbent; and
- do not combine family-lane widening or factorized retention into the selected candidate.

Multi-hue field structure is not the immediate next research unit because current-model pruning recovered
qualifying treatments broadly. It remains deferred if the selected field-retention candidate later fails to
produce a convincing genuinely novel complete-treatment delta.

### No Novel Winner, Novel Slate Alternatives

If final winners are unchanged but genuinely novel treatments reach the retained slate, do not change ranking

- a successful availability repair whose useful alternatives are misordered;
- alternatives that are technically Pareto-valid but not visibly better; and
- remaining field, role, representative, or collapse failures.

Only repeated evidence that a strong retained alternative is systematically misselected justifies a separate
deterministic-ranking research unit. Optional learned ranking remains Phase 6 of the V2 plan and is not the
default response.

If no genuinely novel winner or retained-slate treatment survives the closed selector, do not open a review.

## 0.7.4 Decision Boundary

Version `0.7.4` ends with one of these dispositions:

1. **Candidate supported:** novel final treatments are commonly acceptable or strong, are not systematically
   weaker than the development control, and expose no repeated technical failure. Freeze the deterministic
   candidate and request separate authorization for the V2 Phase 4 fresh directional review.
2. **Ranking question isolated:** strong novel treatments are repeatedly present in the slate but the unchanged
   selector chooses a weaker top-one. Preserve the availability mechanism and begin a separately versioned
   deterministic-ranking diagnostic. Do not fit a learned ranker yet.
3. **Availability mechanism not visibly useful:** novel selected treatments are weak, regress against control,
   or no product-visible delta survives. Keep `0.7.2` as the development baseline and choose one separately
   versioned post-`0.7.4` mechanism from the evidence below.
4. **Mechanical closure fails:** control availability, source lineage, bounds, determinism, obligations, guard,
   or one-factor attribution fails. Repair the closure without opening review or adding another mechanism.

No review result may retroactively select family-lane widening, representative cross-pairing, or factorized
retention inside `0.7.4`.

## Research After 0.7.4

Do not implement the mechanisms below in `0.7.4`. They remain under the V2 plan and require separate versions,
one-factor protocols, generic fixtures, fixed-panel gates, and genuinely novel complete-treatment evidence.
Choose among them only after the closed `0.7.4` winner/slate delta and any authorized bounded review identify
the next failure class.

### Role-Specific Identity Attribution

Field availability is now established well enough to make role-specific attribution a valid later question.
Prioritize it when `0.7.4` review shows that source-connected signature families are available but assigned to
the wrong foreground or accent role, or when important typography, symbols, or repeated marks remain absent
from otherwise defensible fields.

Derive attribution from source geometry, repetition, polarity, component structure, local contrast, and
observability. Generic fixtures must cover dark and light typography, polarity reversals, non-text dark and
light objects, repeated decorative marks, isolated details, and signature accents. Do not encode a literal
foreground color, named artwork, review comment, or desired role assignment.

### Multi-Hue Field Structure

Prioritize piecewise or multi-hue field research when `0.7.4` still cannot represent a visibly important broad
field progression even though current-model proposals are no longer prematurely removed. The mechanism must
derive endpoint families, sequence, topology, and region support from source geometry rather than arbitrary
palette-color combinations.

Generic fixtures should include broad two-stage chromatic transitions, interrupted transitions, hard flat
regions, object-local ramps, fragmented details, stripes, local lighting, and unrelated colorful objects. The
mechanism requires its own architecture version and cannot be combined initially with role-specific
attribution.

### Mechanism-Aware Joint Construction

After either multi-hue fields or role-specific attribution exists, audit whether every defensible new field
hypothesis can coexist with every defensible role direction in a legal complete treatment. Separate loss at
discovery, attribution, representative availability, complete construction, ordinary Pareto, complete-domain
guard, and slate retention. Joint availability does not grant unconditional winner authority.

### Discovery-Semantic Follow-Ups

Consider smaller family bins, iterative family refinement, retaining more components before repetition or
polarity inference, additional gradient angles or centers, denser gradient fitting, or denser contrast
sampling only when custody shows that the needed source direction never reaches the selected field-retention
boundary. Each discovery-semantic change requires an independent protocol and must not be bundled with
another mechanism.

### Deterministic Ranking Research

Ranking research is justified only when complete-treatment review repeatedly shows strong novel alternatives
in the retained slate and weaker top-one selection. Begin with an interpretable deterministic correction and
evaluate the complete generator-to-winner pipeline. Do not use incumbent outputs, review IDs, comments, color
names, source IDs, filenames, or target colors as features. Learned ranking remains optional Phase 6 under the
V2 plan and should be skipped if deterministic ranking is adequate.
