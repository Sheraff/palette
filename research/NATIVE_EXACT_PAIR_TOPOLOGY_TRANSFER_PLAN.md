Status: frozen for `native-exact-pair-topology-transfer-0.1.0-development` implementation and exposed structured-label evaluation. Resource amendment 1 raises only the isolated child RSS ceiling from 1 GiB to 1.25 GiB after the first sealed run stopped before producing artifacts on a 1,151,909,888-byte peak. Eligibility amendment 2 records a `source-ineligible` terminal status after the second sealed run stopped before producing artifacts at the frozen 2.1-million-pixel native decode boundary. Reproducibility amendment 3 immediately freezes each complete projected spatial record after an internal consistency assertion. Reproducibility amendment 4 immediately freezes each complete field-relation evidence record after Black reproduced two graph modes whose only differing leaves were already-computed state-support values with identical serialized inputs. These snapshot barriers prevent cross-record partial writes without changing any computed value. Model logic, evidence, source ordering, coverage denominators, and metric gates are unchanged; concurrency remains two, for a 2.5 GiB aggregate child ceiling.

## Purpose

Audit whether the frozen gradient topology decision transfers from its recorded 224-pixel evidence to the exact same directed endpoint pair after bounded linkage into the current native primary-family partition. This is a transfer audit, not a fitting experiment.

The audit may implement a query-only execution path that produces the same family linkage and topology query as complete graph construction without enumerating relations or hypotheses. It may not change the frozen gradient model, fit a coefficient or threshold, emit a palette, access reserve roots, authorize human review, or authorize promotion.

- Meteora source SHA-256 `26ab93c9c2e806f79e9930f060d39597b1f9ef9811aa19e7ee048903d7e7cb7f` differs in one family at the 448 profile.

The contaminated families record copied `spatial.field` and `spatial.detail` values while retaining their own component summaries. Independent current reconstructions are byte-semantically equal to each other and produce internally consistent family values. The transfer audit must:

1. rerun all 37 frozen graph sources under current code;
2. record every semantic mismatch and classify changed leaf paths;
3. verify independent deterministic reconstruction of every mismatch;
4. verify query-only and complete-graph query equivalence on deterministic fixtures;
5. leave the frozen artifact and every dependent historical artifact unchanged.

The erratum is evidence about reproducibility. It does not retroactively authorize refreezing the graph or recalibrating prior models.

## Query-Only Execution

The query-only path may stop after:

1. native decode and owned native perception;
2. primary-family construction and exact native partition validation;
3. bounded exact/nearest endpoint linkage;
4. construction of the 448, 224, and 112 area-sRGB observation profiles;
5. projection of the unchanged primary masks;
6. exact directed-pair topology evidence and frozen-model decisions.

It must not construct a reduced or alternative family domain. On fixtures, its family queries and topology queries must be deeply equal to complete graph construction for the same inputs.

Each isolated query child has a hard 1.25 GiB peak-RSS ceiling. Exceeding it is a failed run, not a reason to skip or resize a source.

## Evaluation

For every decisive registry row, retain:

- source SHA-256, source root, review batch, family ID, and structured judgment;
- exact directed endpoint RGB values;
- family-linkage status for both endpoints;
- topology status: `mapped`, `ambiguous`, `unmappable`, `indistinguishable`, or `source-ineligible` at the frozen pixel boundary;
- 448, 224, and 112 frozen-model score, margin, and decision when mapped;
- the recorded registry score and decision;
- decision agreement and score delta at 224.

Metrics are reported overall, by class, source root, and temporal review batch. Accuracy and log loss use only mapped rows, while coverage always uses all 274 rows. The registry comparator is recomputed on exactly the mapped subset, not copied from whole-registry metrics.

No label may affect family linkage, evidence construction, source scheduling, or error handling.

## Gates

All gates must pass:

1. all 274 decisive rows and 274 exact source groups match the frozen inventory and source hashes;
2. every row reaches a recorded terminal query status;
3. query-only output equals complete-graph query output on deterministic fixtures;
4. all graph reconstructions and repeated mismatch reconstructions are deterministic;
5. mapped coverage is at least `0.80` overall and at least `0.75` within each label class;
6. mapped 224 accuracy is at least `0.80`, balanced accuracy is at least `0.75`, and mean log loss is below `log(2)`;
7. mapped positive recall is at least `0.80` and mapped negative recall is at least `0.70`;
8. against the registry scorer on the identical mapped subset, accuracy decreases by no more than `0.05` and mean log loss increases by no more than `0.10`;
9. the structured Krafty exact-pair regression is mapped and remains non-gradient at 224;
10. no coefficient, threshold, named-case branch, interpreted comment color, or filename-derived feature enters the audit.

Passing authorizes only consideration of the native exact-pair query as the unchanged gradient component in a future structured field-state plan. It does not authorize complete-tuple inference, palette output, human review, reserve access, or promotion.

## Stop Conditions

Stop and withhold if the registry inventory drifts, any source hash fails, a query-only result differs from complete construction, terminal coverage is incomplete, a metric gate fails, or the Krafty regression returns. Do not tune around a failed source, batch, label class, or named diagnostic.
